A LITTLE GOLD KNOWLEDGE

Three numbers. Three different meanings.

01 / METAL

Melt value

The theoretical value of your gold content at the selected spot price. It excludes workmanship and seller premiums.

02 / BUYING

Store price

Gold value plus craftsmanship, overhead and margin. Compare the quoted price per gram and premium over melt.

03 / SELLING

Buyer payout

A buyer may deduct refining costs and margin. Condition, brand and resale demand can also affect an offer.

How are purity and value calculated?

We use nominal karat fractions: 14K is 14/24, or about 58.33%. A 585 hallmark means 58.5%; use tested fineness for an exact valuation. One troy ounce is 31.1034768 grams.

Does the image show my exact chain?

The chain detail and bust images are style references. Karat colors are illustrative. Images do not predict length, width, weight or construction.

Can I use a store quote to judge a deal?

The checker measures the premium above metal value. It does not authenticate jewelry or determine whether craftsmanship, condition and seller service justify a price. Use a pre-tax quote for a like-for-like comparison.

CUBAN LINK BUYING & SELLING GUIDE

Gold chain questions, answered.

How much is a 14K Cuban link chain worth?

Enter the measured weight and selected gold spot price. This calculator uses 14/24 (about 58.33%) for nominal 14K. The gold-content value is weight × purity × price per gram. A 585 hallmark represents 58.5%; exact valuation requires the actual fineness.

How much should I pay for a Miami Cuban chain?

Select “I’m buying a chain,” then open “Estimate store price + premium.” Adjust the percentage or enter a real seller quote. The result separates metal value from the added premium; it does not certify a fair market price.

How much could I get for selling my gold chain?

Select “I’m selling my gold” to model a buyer’s percentage of melt and any additional fees. The 90% starting value is an editable example, not a prediction. Private resale and collectible jewelry can follow different pricing.

Why is the store price higher than melt value?

A finished chain can include labor, clasp design, finishing, seller overhead, branding and profit. The premium over melt measures their combined price difference. It does not reveal the store’s rent, expenses or net profit.

What is a gold chain premium over melt?

Premium in dollars = seller quote − melt value. Premium percentage = (seller quote ÷ melt value − 1) × 100. This differs from gross margin, which uses selling price as its denominator. Compare pre-tax quotes on the same basis.

How do I compare gold chain prices per gram?

Divide the seller’s pre-tax price by total chain weight. Compare chains with the same karat, construction and relevant design features. The quote checker displays the quoted price per gram alongside the selected karat’s metal value per gram.

How do 10K, 14K and 18K compare?

Nominal purity is karat ÷ 24: 10K is about 41.67%, 14K about 58.33% and 18K 75%. At the same total weight and spot price, higher purity means more gold content. Alloy color and durability also depend on composition and construction.

Does a hollow Cuban chain have the same gold value as a solid one?

At identical measured weight and purity, the gold-content calculation is the same. Hollow and semi-hollow chains can weigh less at the same outside dimensions. The dimension-based builder currently models solid chains only; use a measured weight for hollow pieces.

How do width and length affect Cuban link weight?

Wider and longer chains generally contain more material, but thickness, link spacing, profile, alloy density and clasp change the result. Width and length alone cannot determine exact grams. The builder is calibrated against published handmade Miami specifications (for example, a 12mm 14K 24-inch solid chain at about 265 g); individual makers vary, which is why the estimate is shown as a range.

How do I measure a Cuban chain?

Measure overall length laid straight from clasp to end, and width across the widest link face. Weigh the chain on a suitable jewelry scale. Remove separate pendants and account for stones or non-gold components before using their weight as gold alloy weight.

What is the difference between grams, dwt and troy ounces?

A troy ounce is 31.1034768 grams; one pennyweight (dwt) is 1.55517384 grams. A regular household ounce is a different unit. Choose the unit printed on your scale; this calculator preserves weight when you switch between its supported units. One tola equals 11.6638 grams and is widely used in South Asian gold markets; this calculator supports it too.

What is the difference between 14K and a 585 gold stamp?

14K describes nominal karat purity. A 585 fineness mark specifies 585 parts of gold per thousand, or 58.5%. The difference matters for precise valuation. A stamp by itself does not verify authenticity or replace testing.

Does this calculator include diamonds, tax or shipping?

Melt value covers the entered gold-alloy weight and purity only. The buying scenario is pre-tax and excludes shipping. Do not value diamond weight as gold; gemstones and separate components require their own assessment.

Is the displayed price a real jewelry listing?

No. This website provides calculations and editable scenarios. The chain images are style references, not inventory for sale. Only a quote entered from an actual seller represents that seller’s asking price.

What happens if the gold price feed is unavailable?

The website labels the feed status and retains the displayed editable price. A fallback price is not current market data. Enter a current reference price and confirm its currency and troy-ounce unit before comparing offers.
