What to write about

  • Corrections. If a number, conversion, or statement on the site looks wrong, say so — accuracy is the entire point of this site, and corrections are acted on.
  • Specification data. If you are a maker or jeweler and a published chain specification differs from what our weight estimator assumes, send the specification. Real maker data improves the model.
  • Calculator feedback. Bugs, confusing labels, or a gold item the calculator handles badly.
  • Business inquiries. Partnerships and advertising questions related to the site.

What we cannot do by email

This site is a calculator and an educational resource, not a jeweler or an appraiser. We cannot appraise or authenticate individual pieces of jewelry, verify whether a specific item is genuine, or tell you whether a specific deal is right for you. We also do not buy or sell gold. For an appraisal or authentication, a local jeweler with testing equipment — XRF or acid testing — can examine the actual piece. For everything the calculator can answer, start with the calculator and the FAQ.

We read every message, but as a small site we cannot guarantee a reply or a response time. If your message is about privacy or your data, mention that in the subject line and it will be prioritized. See the privacy policy for how this site handles information.
