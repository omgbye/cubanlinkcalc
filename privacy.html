SITE POLICY

Privacy policy.

Last updated: October 2026. This policy describes what information is collected when you use cubanlinkcalc.com, how it is used, and the choices you have. This site is a calculator and a set of educational guides; it has no user accounts and sells nothing.

Information we collect

Calculator inputs are processed in your browser. The weights, karats, prices, and quotes you type into the calculator are used only to perform calculations on your own device. They are not transmitted to us, not stored by us, and not linked to your identity.

Usage data via analytics. Like most websites, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site in aggregate — which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors are located, what devices and browsers are used, and how visitors arrive at the site. This data is collected by Google on our behalf and is reported to us in aggregated form.

Server logs. The site is served by a hosting provider that keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and operations. We do not combine these logs with calculator inputs, which we never receive.

If you contact us. If you email us through the address on the contact page, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, and we use them only to respond to you.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-MGXCDLBHK6) to measure traffic and improve the site. Google Analytics sets cookies (including the _ga cookie) to distinguish visitors and sessions, and collects information such as your IP address (which Google may truncate or anonymize depending on region), device and browser details, and pages viewed. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics across all websites by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, and you can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings; the calculator will continue to work without analytics cookies.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This site may display advertising served by Google AdSense. In connection with that advertising:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, users can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Some ads on this site may also be served by other third-party advertising vendors or networks, which may use cookies or similar technologies subject to their own privacy policies. If personalized advertising is disabled, you will still see ads, but they will be based on the content of the page rather than on your browsing history.

Live gold price data

The calculator displays a live gold price. To provide it, your browser requests the current price directly from the gold-api.com price service (and a public gold price feed for the day-change indicator). Those requests go from your browser to those services and are handled under their respective terms and privacy practices. We do not send them any information about you or about the values you calculate.

Cookies

A cookie is a small text file stored by your browser. This site itself sets no first-party cookies for the calculator's operation. Cookies on this site come from the third-party services described above: Google Analytics (measurement), Google AdSense and its partners (advertising), and your browser's own handling of embedded content. You can view, block, and delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Data retention and security

Analytics data is retained in Google Analytics according to our configured retention settings and Google's policies. Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your inquiry. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit what we collect in the first place — the most sensitive data in this context, your calculations, never leaves your device.

Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience educational tool about gold pricing. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site's features or the services it uses change. The current version will always be posted on this page, with the month and year of the most recent update shown at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
