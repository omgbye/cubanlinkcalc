Financial & Valuation Disclaimer.

Last updated: October 2026. Important disclosures regarding commodity spot feeds, jewelry modeling limits, and why calculations are educational estimates.

Summary: Cuban Link Calculator is an independent educational tool. Calculations provided on this website are estimates based on public formulas and live market feeds. Nothing on this website constitutes financial, investment, or certified jewelry appraisal advice.

1. Not a Certified Jewelry Appraisal

The calculations, weights, and valuations generated by cubanlinkcalc.com do not constitute a formal, certified, or legal jewelry appraisal. An authentic valuation requires an in-person physical inspection by a credentialed jeweler or gemologist using calibrated digital scales, specific gravity testing, acid testing, or X-ray fluorescence (XRF) spectrometry. A hallmark stamp (e.g., 10K, 14K, 18K, 585, 750) is an indicator of claimed purity but is not proof of authenticity or solid construction.

2. Spot Market Volatility and Data Latency

Precious metal spot prices trade continuously on international exchanges. While our calculator fetches live market quotes (via live market and currency feeds), spot prices fluctuate second by second. Feed outages, internet latency, and market closures may cause quotes to differ from the exact spot price at the precise second of your transaction. You can manually adjust the spot price input field at any time to reflect the reference price you are being quoted.

3. Dimension Modeling vs. Physical Weight

Our solid Cuban link build estimator models chain weight from length and width based on standardized geometric density formulas for solid gold alloys. However, real handmade and machine-cut chains vary widely in physical specifications:

  • Link Tightness and Filing: How tightly the links are joined and the depth of the diamond-cut face filing will increase or decrease total gram weight.
  • Clasp Construction: Box locks, double-figure-eight safety clasps, and custom hinges add varying amounts of metal and may contain steel safety springs.
  • Hollow and Semi-Solid Chains: The dimension estimator assumes a 100% solid gold chain. If a chain is hollow or electroformed, its true weight will be far lower than the solid dimension estimate.

A calibrated physical scale reading always supersedes any mathematical dimension estimate.

4. Buying Markups and Scrap Gold Payouts

The buying and selling scenarios provided on this site are illustrative math models:

  • Retail Quotes: Jewelers add varying premiums over melt value to account for labor, craftsmanship, design, retail overhead, and profit. A retail quote is not a statement of the metal's resale worth.
  • Scrap Payouts: Refiners and scrap buyers do not pay 100% of the melt value; they deduct refining and assay fees and their operating costs, so payouts land below melt value — how far below varies by buyer, which is why comparing net offers matters.
